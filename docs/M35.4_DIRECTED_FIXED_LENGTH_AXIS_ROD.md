# M35.4 Directed Fixed-Length 3D Axis Rod

M35.4 is a diagnostic layer on top of the existing foam-ring grasp geometry.
It projects a centered, fixed-physical-length 3-D rod along
`ring_axis_toward_camera` into the RGB image.

## Contract

- Only successfully paired ring/mouth instances with a valid 3-D center and axis are rendered.
- The total rod length is configured by `axis_direction.rod_length_mm`; its 2-D length also depends on depth and perspective position.
- The positive signed axis half is encoded as the near, solid red arrow.
- The negative half is encoded as the far, cyan dashed segment.
- The diagnostic JSON records 3-D endpoints, 2-D endpoints, projected length and depth ordering.
- `axis_direction.enabled=false` skips the M35.4 endpoint/projection/render/output path and removes stale diagnostic files.
- The M35.2 core pose normal is not disabled because grasp-frame and collision logic depend on it.
