# M35.1 Complete Gripper Geometry and Static Final-Pose Collision

## Scope

M35.1 extends M34_new.4 with a measured full end-effector model and final-pose collision checks. It does **not** implement dynamic travel/open/insert/close/lift sweeps.

## Measurement precedence

The newest measurements supplied for M35.1 override older approximations:

- contact block: 15 × 20 × 35 mm;
- moving finger: pivot-to-tip 90 mm, 16 mm closing-axis width, 20 mm lateral thickness;
- inner gap range: 10–75 mm;
- pivot-center separation: 36 mm;
- pivot center 55 mm below mounting-disk lower surface;
- palm: 50 × 35 × 60 mm;
- mounting disk: Ø70 × 20 mm;
- pneumatic fitting: center 25 mm below disk lower surface, 30 mm protrusion, Ø10 mm;
- wrist approximation: Ø100 × 200 mm;
- foam ring: ID 60 mm, OD 85 mm, local wall 14 mm, axial length 70 mm;
- intended contact-block insertion: 15–20 mm; default remains 20 mm.

## Model

New module:

```text
production/foam_ring_grasp/tasks/foam_ring_grasp_vision/gripper_model_3d.py
```

Collision primitives:

- two oriented moving-finger OBBs;
- two contact-block OBBs;
- palm OBB;
- mounting-disk cylinder;
- pneumatic-fitting cylinder;
- robot-wrist cylinder.

The final finger angle is reconstructed from the measured symmetric pivot geometry and commanded inner gap.

## Static collision checks

For every valid 12-clock candidate, the complete final-pose model is checked against:

1. the calibrated 3-D box model;
2. visible aligned-depth point clouds of all other foam-ring instances.

New candidate field:

```text
full_gripper_static
```

New hard-rejection reasons:

```text
full_gripper_static_model_unconfigured
full_gripper_static_box_unconfigured
full_gripper_static_box_collision
full_gripper_static_box_clearance_too_small
full_gripper_static_neighbor_collision
full_gripper_static_neighbor_clearance_too_small
full_gripper_static_neighbor_unknown
```

## Robot-side frame

Besides the existing fingertip grasp frame, M35.1 outputs:

```text
mounting_interface_frame_camera
```

This frame is located at the center of the mounting disk's upper surface and uses the same orientation as the grasp frame. It remains expressed in the camera optical frame; hand-eye and robot tool transforms are not applied by the visual system.

## Known assumption

The pneumatic fitting is modelled on the gripper `+Y` side because the photos do not establish a robot-frame sign convention. Change:

```yaml
pneumatic_fitting:
# M35.2.2: side-mounted on the gripper right face; hidden in the front view,
# horizontally separated in the side view.
  axis: negative_y
```

if the final tool-frame definition places it on the opposite side.
