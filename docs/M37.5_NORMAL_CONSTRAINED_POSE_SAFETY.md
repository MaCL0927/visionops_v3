# M37.5 Cylinder-Surface Separation, Normal-Constrained Axis Fitting, and Pose Rejection

## Goal

M37.5 addresses a failure mode observed in M37.4: a side-ring cylinder could
obtain a low fixed-radius residual while its axis was visibly wrong. Dense
contact between rings, end-face points, inner-wall points, depth jumps and
D2C edge mixing made the purely radial objective geometrically ambiguous.

The production rule is now safety-first: uncertain pose is rejected and the
same depth layer continues with another target. A visually plausible radial
fit is no longer sufficient to publish a grasp point.

## M36 mouth-visible safety gate

The M36 ellipse-derived normal and depth-plane normal are compared before a
candidate can become eligible. The pair is rejected when:

- normal disagreement exceeds `maximum_normal_disagreement_deg`;
- an ellipse-stabilized pose has weak depth-plane support and material cue
  disagreement;
- the stabilized plane residual is above its hard threshold.

The original warning remains, but large disagreement now produces explicit
rejection reasons rather than a forced axis.

## M37 organized surface separation

For each target `foam_ring`, M37.5 operates inside its bbox and performs:

1. mask erosion;
2. removal of all other ring masks, with configurable dilation around contact
   zones;
3. removal of pixels adjacent to a depth discontinuity;
4. removal of tiny disconnected surface islands;
5. organized RGB-D deprojection;
6. local normal calculation from a cross-shaped 3-D neighborhood.

Normals are oriented toward the camera for diagnostics. The cylinder objective
uses sign-independent dot products.

## Normal-constrained axis hypotheses

Axis seeds are generated from:

- the minimum-eigenvalue direction of the surface-normal covariance;
- cross products of robustly sampled normal pairs;
- point-cloud PCA directions;
- a small fallback hemisphere set.

Unlike the legacy 80/320-direction blind search, M37.5 first ranks directions
by whether surface normals are perpendicular to the candidate axis.

The cylinder score combines:

- fixed-radius point residual;
- side-normal perpendicularity to the axis;
- observed-normal alignment with the fitted radial direction;
- normal inlier ratio;
- visible cylindrical normal angular span.

The best three distinct axes are retained through local refinement.

## Pose uncertainty rejection

A side-ring pose is rejected when any configured safety gate fails, including:

- too few valid surface normals;
- low normal inlier ratio;
- large normal-axis or normal-radial angular error;
- insufficient visible normal span;
- two distant axis hypotheses with an insufficient score margin;
- disagreement between final axis and covariance normal seed;
- unstable axis under repeated normal bootstrap resampling.

The output contains `pose_uncertainty`, all retained hypotheses, score margin,
axis separation, seed disagreement and bootstrap angles.

## Neighbor exclusion

The online hybrid path passes the union of all other `foam_ring` masks to the
M37 fitter. Only target eligibility is restricted; M36/M37 scene-level depth
layering and collision visibility remain unchanged.

## Compatibility

- M37.4 depth-layer ordering is retained.
- A trigger still performs at most one warm-start local accurate refinement.
- The C++ Runtime, RKNN model, RGB-D cache and request protocol are unchanged.
- `scene.m37_4_timing` is retained as a compatibility alias.
- New summaries are available under `scene.m37_5_pose_safety` and
  `scene.m37_5_timing`.

## Board validation

After updating Python files and restarting the online service:

```bash
cd /opt/visionops_v3
bash production/foam_ring_grasp/scripts/start_online_service.sh
```

Trigger with debug output and inspect the latest result:

```bash
curl -s -X POST http://127.0.0.1:19213/api/foam_ring/infer_once \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"request_id":"m37-5-001","wait":true,"timeout_ms":20000,"save_debug":true}' \
  | python3 -m json.tool

python3 production/foam_ring_grasp/scripts/summarize_hybrid_timing.py
```

Offline replay:

```bash
python3 production/foam_ring_grasp/scripts/replay_m37_5_result_set.py \
  /path/to/foam_ring_online_geometry \
  --output /tmp/m37_5_replay
```
