# M37.4 Replay Validation on 16 Sequential Grasp Captures

Source: user-provided `result_16.tar.gz`.

The saved Runtime polygons, exact RGB-D images and camera intrinsics were replayed
through M37.4. Box calibration was resolved to the repository-local
`production/foam_ring_grasp/config/box_model.json` so M36 collision results were
reproduced correctly.

## Ordering

- Historical successful selections: 15
- Historical selections from layer 0: 6
- Historical selections from deeper layers: 9
- M37.4 successful selections: 15
- M37.4 selections from layer 0: 14
- M37.4 selections from layer 1 after layer-0 failure: 1
- M37.4 selections below layer 1: 0

The one layer-1 selection occurred only after the nearest layer could not produce
an accepted candidate.

## Bounded refinement

Across all 16 replays:

- maximum fast attempts in one trigger: 3
- triggers using local accurate refinement: 6
- maximum local accurate refinements in one trigger: 1
- full global accurate searches used by the online hybrid path: 0

## Timing

Historical hybrid branch timing measured on RK3576 from the saved results:

- mean: 4843.3 ms
- median: 1372.0 ms
- p95: 14975.3 ms
- maximum: 15041.8 ms

M37.4 replay hybrid timing on the local validation machine:

- mean: 312.3 ms
- median: 385.9 ms
- p95: 470.8 ms
- maximum: 591.0 ms

These timing groups are not directly comparable hardware benchmarks. The local
replay validates algorithmic boundedness: no full global accurate fallback and
at most one local refinement. Final RK3576 p50/p95 must be measured after board
deployment.
